## flexera-cli finops-billing invoice-schedule-invoices list

Index invoices generated by a schedule

### Synopsis

Index invoices generated by a schedule

Lists invoices generated by the specified invoice schedule.

The invoiceNumber query parameter filters on an exact, case-sensitive invoice number. invoiceNumber is also accepted in orderBy, ascending and descending. Invoices sort numerically by their allocated sequence number; invoices without a number come last in both directions, and ties are broken by id.

flexera-cli finops-billing invoice-schedule-invoices list [flags]
